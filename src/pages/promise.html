Safety standards

AGSC/AGRSS principles.
Applied with care.

The Auto Glass Safety Council (AGSC) publishes the ANSI/AGSC/AGRSS 005 standard for proper auto glass replacement. The practices below describe the safety-focused workflow Integrity Glass should follow; confirm each step reflects current shop procedures.

Read the AGSC/AGRSS standardView the AGSC compliance checklist
01

Check the vehicle requirements

Identify the exact vehicle, glass configuration, applicable installation instructions, and any manufacturer-specific requirements before work begins.

02

Use compatible materials and procedures

Confirm the replacement glass, adhesives, preparation steps, and handling instructions for the application; observe product instructions and safe-drive-away guidance.

03

Address ADAS when required

Check whether windshield replacement affects cameras or sensors, follow applicable vehicle-maker calibration procedures, and confirm the outcome with the customer.

04

Keep the work clear and accountable

Explain repair versus replacement options, timing, and any calibration needs. Ask for the service details and warranty terms in writing.

AGSC technician certification, registered-company membership, and independent validation are distinct programs. This page describes Integrity Glass’s intended safety approach; it does not claim an unverified AGSC credential, membership, or audit. The shop should verify each listed step reflects current procedures before publishing. Confirm technician credentials and job-specific procedures directly before service.
