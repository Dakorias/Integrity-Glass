Safety standards

AGSC/AGRSS principles.
Applied with care.

The Auto Glass Safety Council (AGSC) publishes the ANSI/AGSC/AGRSS 005 standard for proper auto glass replacement. The practices below describe the safety-focused workflow Integrity Glass should follow; confirm each step reflects current shop procedures.

01Confirm vehicle requirements+

Identify the vehicle, glass configuration, and applicable manufacturer instructions before selecting a repair or replacement approach.

02Check materials and safe-drive-away time+

Adhesive selection, handling, cure time, and safe-drive-away guidance depend on the product, conditions, and vehicle procedure. Confirm the instructions that apply to the job.

03Check camera and sensor requirements+

Determine whether glass service affects driver-assistance cameras or sensors. Follow the applicable manufacturer calibration procedure and explain any required next steps.

04Review the work and documentation+

Before work begins, review the estimate, timing, and service details. Ask for safe-drive-away guidance and any applicable calibration records in writing.
