Some vehicles require camera or sensor calibration after windshield replacement or related work. Requirements vary, so the vehicle information and manufacturer procedures need to be checked for each job.

Service 03

Rock-chip repair

A rock-chip repair fills and stabilizes eligible impact damage. Results depend on the chip type, size, age, cleanliness, and position; repair may remain visible and cannot be guaranteed to make glass look new.

What we’ll check

  • Chip type, size, age, and cleanliness
  • Location relative to the edge and driver view
  • Whether the damage has begun to spread

Custom-cut glass

Custom-cut glass starts with the opening, intended application, glass type, thickness, edge details, and any safety or regulatory requirements. Confirm feasibility and specifications before ordering.

What we’ll check

  • Exact measurements and template
  • Application, material, thickness, and edge finish
  • Applicable safety-glazing requirements

Heavy equipment installation

Equipment glass can differ by machine, cab, model year, and operating environment. Provide the equipment make, model, serial details if available, and glass location to confirm parts and installation options.

What we’ll check

  • Equipment make, model, and serial information
  • Cab opening, curves, seals, and mounting method

Service 06

Side & rear glass

Side and rear glass often need replacement rather than repair. Share which panel is damaged and whether the vehicle is secure so the correct part and service plan can be identified.

What we’ll check

  • Which panel is damaged
  • Tint, defrost, antenna, and trim features
  • Safe temporary covering and cleanup needs

Safety comes first

Glass is part of your vehicle’s safety system.

Windshields help support the vehicle structure and may house cameras and sensors. If replacement is needed, confirm whether your vehicle requires calibration and how that is handled before scheduling.

For a useful first assessment, share the year, make, model, trim, damage location, and a clear photo if available.

For windshield replacement, we check vehicle-specific installation requirements and whether the vehicle’s driver-assistance systems need calibration. Ask what procedure and documentation apply to your vehicle.
