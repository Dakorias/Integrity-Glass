Can every chip or crack be repaired?+

No. Size, type, location, and how long the damage has been exposed all matter. Damage near the edge or in the driver’s direct sightline may need replacement. A technician can review the details and explain the options.

How do I know if I need repair or replacement?+

A technician assesses the damage and the vehicle’s glass requirements. Share clear photos, your vehicle year/make/model, and where the damage sits on the glass to help start that conversation.

Do you offer mobile appointments?+

Mobile availability depends on the address, vehicle, weather, and work required. Include your ZIP code with a quote request so current coverage can be confirmed.

What is the service area?+

Middle Georgia is shown as a sample service area on this site. Confirm your exact ZIP code and availability with Integrity Glass before scheduling.

Will my vehicle need camera calibration?+

Some vehicles require driver-assistance camera or sensor calibration after windshield replacement. Provide the exact year, make, model, and trim so the technician can confirm what applies.

What warranty coverage is available?+

Ask for the current written terms, including what is covered, exclusions, and how to request warranty service.

Can I use insurance?+

Coverage and deductibles vary by policy. Contact your insurer to confirm benefits and claim requirements; Integrity Glass can discuss the information needed for a quote.

How do I get an accurate price?+

Send the vehicle year, make, model, trim, damaged glass location, ZIP code, and any sensor or camera details you know. The listed ranges are examples only and are not a confirmed quote.

Can you calibrate ADAS after windshield replacement?+

ADAS calibration is available for applicable service needs. Whether a vehicle requires static, dynamic, or another manufacturer-specified procedure depends on the vehicle. Confirm the required procedure and documentation when requesting an estimate.

Do you repair rock chips?+

Rock-chip repair is available when the damage is a suitable candidate. Chip size, location, type, and condition affect eligibility, and some repair marks can remain visible.

Do you install custom-cut glass or heavy equipment glass?+

Custom-cut glass and heavy equipment installations are listed services. Feasibility depends on measurements, application, equipment details, glass specifications, and applicable safety requirements; contact the shop to confirm.

Is Integrity Glass AGSC certified or registered?+

AGSC technician certification, registered-company status, and independent validation are separate programs. Ask Integrity Glass to confirm its current participation and technician credentials before booking. This website does not claim an unverified AGSC membership or certification.
