Can every chip or crack be repaired?+

No. Size, type, location, and how long the damage has been exposed all matter. Damage near the edge or in the driver’s direct sightline may need replacement. A technician can review the details and explain the options.

How do I know if I need repair or replacement?+

A technician assesses the damage and the vehicle’s glass requirements. Share your vehicle details, clear photos, and where the damage sits on the glass to help start that conversation.

Do you repair rock chips?+

Rock-chip repair may be an option for suitable damage. Chip size, location, type, and condition affect eligibility; some repair marks can remain visible.

Will my vehicle need camera calibration?+

Some vehicles require driver-assistance camera or sensor calibration after windshield replacement. Provide the exact year, make, model, and trim so the technician can confirm what applies.

Can ADAS be calibrated after windshield replacement?+

Requirements vary by vehicle. A manufacturer may specify static, dynamic, or another procedure. Confirm the required procedure and documentation when requesting an estimate.

Do you offer mobile appointments?+

Mobile availability depends on address, vehicle, weather, and work required. Include your ZIP code so current coverage can be confirmed.

What is the service area?+

Integrity Glass serves Middle Georgia. Confirm your exact ZIP code and appointment availability before scheduling.

Can I use insurance?+

Coverage and deductibles vary by policy. Contact your insurer to confirm benefits and claim requirements; Integrity Glass can discuss the information needed for a quote.

How do I get an accurate price?+

Share vehicle year, make, model, trim, damaged glass location, ZIP code, and any sensor or camera details you know. Published ranges are examples only, not a confirmed quote.

What warranty coverage is available?+

Ask for the current written terms, including what is covered, exclusions, and how to request warranty service.

Do you install custom-cut glass or heavy equipment glass?+

Custom-cut glass and heavy equipment installation are listed services. Feasibility depends on measurements, application, equipment details, glass specifications, and safety requirements; contact the shop to confirm.

Is Integrity Glass AGSC certified or registered?+

AGSC technician certification, registered-company status, and independent validation are separate programs. Ask Integrity Glass to confirm current participation and technician credentials before booking. This website does not claim an unverified AGSC membership or certification.
